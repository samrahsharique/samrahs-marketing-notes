Marketing Insight of the Week

MARKETING INSIGHT

Most campaigns win attention.
Very few win memory.

Most campaigns optimise for immediate visibility but ignore how memory actually forms. Distinctive brand assets, emotional encoding, and repeated category entry point exposure are what drive long-term brand recall. Without memory structures, even high-reach campaigns decay faster than marketers expect.

Strategic takeaway: optimise for memory, not just clicks.
